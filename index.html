About Us

Mission & Strategy

Our Mission

To revolutionize competitive table tennis in Puerto Rico through professional-grade tournament management, cutting-edge technology integration, and unwavering commitment to ITTF standards — giving every player, league, and event the visibility and operational excellence they deserve.

Our Strategy

Leverage proprietary software and data-driven operations to deliver seamless tournament experiences. From automated seeding and bracket generation to real-time digital results through platforms like StadiumCompete, we bridge the gap between grassroots competition and world-class event production.

Our Vision

Position Puerto Rico as a premier destination for competitive table tennis in the Caribbean — building a sustainable ecosystem where players develop, sponsors engage, and the sport grows through technology-enhanced transparency and professionalism.

Our Values

  • ◆ Operational excellence under international standards
  • ◆ Technology as a competitive advantage, not a gimmick
  • ◆ Player-first experiences at every touchpoint
  • ◆ Transparent, data-driven decision making
